About Us

The ShowWise story

ShowWise started as a software company — building tools to help event professionals manage productions more efficiently at showwise.app. But we're not just coders. We're people who've worked events.

ShowWise Events is the natural extension of that — the boots-on-the-ground arm where we take everything we know about events and apply it directly. We consult, we crew, we manage, we operate.

We're based in Canberra and work across the ACT and surrounding regions day-to-day, but our consulting practice takes us further — nationally and internationally when the work calls for it.
